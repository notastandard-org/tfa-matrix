Upstream frameworks and sources

The TFA Matrix builds directly on the following open frameworks and organisations.

  • MITRE ATT&CK® — The structural model — tactics, techniques, matrices, STIX representation — is adapted from MITRE ATT&CK. The TFA Matrix applies that model to interpersonal harm.
  • OASIS Open — STIX 2.1 — The STIX 2.1 data format that underpins the machine-readable representation of the framework.
  • Coalition Against Stalkerware — Detection tooling and victim-support guidance referenced in mitigations for monitoring-app techniques.
